About Mag-aso falls

Mag-Aso Falls is a natural waterfall attraction in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental (Philippines) known for its scenic beauty and refreshing waters. It’s one of the popular tourist spots in the area, especially for nature lovers and day-trippers.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Brgy. Oringao, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental
  • Best Time to Visit: Summer (March–May)
  • Type: Natural Waterfall
  • Water Feature: Twin Falls with cool, clear pool
  • Activities: Swimming, Sightseeing, Picnicking, Photography

Why Visit?

Mag-aso Falls is worth visiting because of its beautiful natural scenery, cool and clear water, and peaceful environment. It is a perfect place to relax, swim, and enjoy nature away from the busy city.
